# Organizational Asset Catalog Synthetic Data

This document describes the deterministic catalog generated by `scripts/build_organizational_asset_catalog.py` and promoted on September 30, 2026. Values are synthetic and intended for ArcGIS Dashboard, relationship, and knowledge-graph demonstrations. They are not an asserted inventory.

For the end-to-end relationship among the source, enriched Vertex output, and organization catalog, begin with [Catalog and Enrichment Guide](catalog_and_enrichment_guide.md).

The facilities are Vertex Medical Center, Point Place, Line Lake, Node North, Rohdran Cove, and Admin HQ. The four named hospitals and Admin HQ are placed at deterministic synthetic geographic anchors. Facility metadata records the anchor city separately from the facility name.

Vertex Medical Center is imported from the reviewed `Vertex_Assets_Enriched.gdb`. Its asset IDs, geometry, lifecycle values, inspections, repairs, work orders, and narrative text are preserved; only geodatabase-specific relationship GUIDs are remapped. The other five facilities are generated by the catalog builder. Use the standalone enriched geodatabase for a Vertex-only demonstration, or apply `Facility_ID = 'VMC'` to the catalog's asset and lifecycle layers.

The four generated hospitals use the generic Hospital profile. Admin HQ uses the Government Office profile. A standard-library-only planner creates distinct rotated corridor-and-room layouts, room programs, names, capacities, and stable hierarchy IDs; ArcPy converts those rectangles to polygons and fits each Facility inside its translated Site. No CAD/BIM file, hand-authored room list, third-party geometry package, or additional software is required. These plans are plausible synthetic layouts, not reconstructions of real buildings.

Generated Facilities receive facility-specific addresses, hours, heights, level counts, and cleared image fields. Generated Units receive capacities and amenity flags from their room program. They do not inherit Vertex Exam Room equipment flags, Vertex photo URLs, or Vertex access hours from the schema templates.

The promoted catalog and manifest contain the figures above. Validation found all expected parent and relationship references and zero orphans.

## Lifecycle Distribution

The complete organization receives an exact deterministic asset-age split, subject only to integer rounding:

| Lifecycle stage | Share | Asset age as share of expected life |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Recently Installed | 20% | At least 184 days; no more than 2 years or 25% of service life |
| Within Service Life | 50% | 20% through 75% |
| Retrofit/Replacement | 30% | 80% through 105% |

Facility-level mixes vary slightly so the six sites do not look statistically identical, while the organization-wide 20/50/30 requirement remains exact.

The validated organization-wide counts are 970 recently installed, 2,426 within service life, and 1,456 at retrofit/replacement.

## Narrative Generation Result

Deterministic code generated all authoritative values. The local Mistral model was offered only bounded synthetic facts for short narrative fields. The promoted run encountered 5,912 narrative opportunities, sent the configured maximum of 500 requests, accepted 498 responses, used two request-error fallbacks, and used deterministic budget fallback for 5,412 remaining opportunities. A fallback changes narrative wording only; it does not affect IDs, dates, costs, conditions, cohorts, or relationships.

## Relationship Model

- Facilities have one catalog metadata record and many exterior assets.
- Units host many indoor asset points.
- Each indoor point has one lifecycle record.
- Lifecycle assets have many inspections, repairs, work orders, and system dependencies.
- GlobalIDs and GUID foreign keys are authoritative relationship keys.
- Stable business identifiers drive deterministic generation and remain available for dashboards and import/export.
- External-reference, field-work, and media tables intentionally remain empty until real ArcGIS Online or enterprise item identifiers exist.

## Validation Contract

A successful run verifies the exact Site and Facility sets, including the intentional `VMC` Facility to `MMC` Site relationship; complete Facility, Level, and Unit parentage; planned room IDs, names, and use types; non-null contained geometry; asset business-key continuity; assets at all six facilities; exact lifecycle cohort totals; inspection date bounds; latest-inspection consistency; relationship foreign keys; exact preservation of imported Vertex records; and zero fabricated integration/media links. The promoted manifest records 1,025 Units, 4,852 lifecycle assets, 72 exterior assets, 1,003 preserved Vertex assets, three direct asset-history relationships, and zero orphan references.
